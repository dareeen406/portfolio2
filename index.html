Inc. 5000 Companies — Data Cleaning & Business Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes data from 5,000 Inc. 5000 companies to identify business patterns and generate useful insights.

The analysis includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualization, and business insights.

Objectives

  • Identify the industries with the highest number of companies.
  • Compare total revenue across industries.
  • Identify the states with the highest number of companies.
  • Find the companies with the highest revenue and growth.
  • Examine the relationship between company size and revenue.

Libraries Used

  • Pandas — for data manipulation and analysis.
  • Matplotlib — for data visualization.

Business Insights

The following insights summarize the key findings from the analysis.

Business Insight 1

IT Services has the highest number of companies in the dataset, with 733 companies.

Business Insight 2

The Health industry has the highest total revenue in the dataset, generating approximately $22.02 billion.

Business Insight 3

California has the highest number of companies in the dataset, with 694 companies.

Business Insight 4

Pacific Union Real Estate has the highest revenue among the companies in the dataset, with approximately $5.53 billion.

Business Insight 5

There is a weak positive relationship between the number of employees and revenue, suggesting that larger companies tend to have higher revenue, but employee count alone does not strongly explain revenue differences.
